Photography

TimeLine

Around 400 BC

The earliest ideas appear. These are camera obscura image projection and the observation that some substances change when exposed to light.

16th century

Leonardo da Vinci sketches instructions for building a camera obscura.

1826-1827

Nicéphore Niépce creates the first permanent photograph.

1838

Louis Daguerre takes “Boulevard du Temple,” the first picture to include a human.

1839

The daguerreotype is introduced, the first commercially viable photographic process. Some sources treat 19 August 1839 as the birthday of photography.

1843

John Quincy Adams becomes the first known U.S. president to sit for a photograph, taken by Philip Haas.

1858

Nadar takes the first aerial photograph.

1862

French inventors Louis Ducos du Hauron and Charles Cros, working independently, begin patenting color photography methods.

1880

The Daily Graphic becomes the first U.S. newspaper to publish a photomechanical reproduction of a photograph.

1888

George Eastman launches the first Kodak camera.

1907

The first practical color plate reaches the market.
