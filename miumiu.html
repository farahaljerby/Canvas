Miu Miu: The Poetry of the Human Body

“The clothes that are closest to you.”

The smallness of our human bodies, in the vastness of our world. Miuccia Prada chooses to return to the authentic nature from which everything was created, to the raw and real aspect of fashion born out of utility, to the instinctive need to choose clothing as a way of making oneself small in a boundless world.

For this Fall/Winter 2026 collection, Miu Miu, Miuccia Prada rethinks the concept of dressing. At the beginning of human history, clothing was not born as an aesthetic choice but as an instinct for survival—for protection, defence, or camouflage. In this sense, it can be considered an almost animal gesture, precisely because it is an instinctive, immediate, and real response. Everything in this collection suggests a raw narrative choice: the atmosphere evokes a modern countryside, with green grass and moss, and furs enveloping the space. The body becomes small and delicate in the face of the vastness of the world we face every day; it becomes a warrior of challenges and difficulties, finding new strength and courage. Miuccia Prada’s discourse continues, and her investigation into what it means to be a woman today adds a new chapter to the brand’s stylistic research, placing the act of dressing at the center of it all.

Silhouettes and materials play with contrasts of a complex and layered femininity, combining innocence with rebellion, fragility with strength. Female identity is not singular but presents multiple distinct facets: it changes, dialogues, evolves, returns, and disappears. The relationship between the body and clothing is central; fashion is not merely decoration but the construction of a personal identity, a true contemporary cultural statement.
